# Homepage Override

## Purpose
Polished ecommerce home page focused on fast discovery, trustworthy product presentation, and a strong primary shopping action.

## Layout
1. Compact service topbar.
2. Sticky glass-like navigation.
3. Hero with value proposition, one primary CTA, secondary browse action, trust chips, and a lightweight product mockup.
4. Category grid with icon + label + product count.
5. Featured products with search and responsive card grid.
6. Promotional banner with explicit coupon code.
7. Trust/benefit cards.
8. Footer with navigation and cart summary.

## Visual behavior
- Keep emerald as the dominant brand signal.
- Use orange only for urgency/discount/action accents.
- Use restrained gradients/blur in the hero and nav; avoid decorative blur over content.
- Keep cards readable on white surfaces.
- Use hover lift/opacity/transform within a short transition window.
- Preserve final readable state for reduced motion.

## Content behavior
- Product search is client-side on the current demo catalog.
- Cart count and total are updated live and announced to assistive technology.
- Empty search results are explicit rather than silently leaving a blank area.
